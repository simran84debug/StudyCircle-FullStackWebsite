HOW TO BALANCE SCHOOL AND COACHING

Balancing school and coaching can be challenging, but with effective time management and prioritization, it is possible to excel in both. treat your school hours as warm-up sessions for your coaching classes. Do all non-core homework (like English or Physical Education) at school. At home, focus solely on coaching concepts and multiple-choice questions (MCQs). Here are some tips to help you manage your time effectively:

Align your topics:

Ensure the topics you study at school match the topics in your coaching. This helps you learn the concept once and revise it twice.

Prioritize your tasks:

Identify the most important tasks and focus on them first.

Use a planner:

Create a schedule that includes time for school, coaching, and self-study.

Take breaks:

Allow yourself short breaks to rest and recharge.

Stay organized:

Keep your study materials and notes organized to save time.

Use weekends wisely:

Dedicate Saturdays and Sundays to taking mock tests, doing a weekly review, and catching up on any missed work.

Seek help when needed:

Don't hesitate to ask teachers or peers for help if you're struggling with a topic. Remember, consistency is key. Stick to your schedule and make adjustments as needed to ensure you are effectively balancing both school and coaching.
