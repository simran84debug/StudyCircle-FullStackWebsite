Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Find answers to common questions about our courses, registration process, and more.

1. How can I register for a course?

Click on the Register button, create your account, fill in the required details, choose your preferred course, and complete the enrollment process.

2. What courses does Study Circle offer?

We offer coaching for JEE, NEET, Olympiads, Classes 6-12, foundation courses, board exam preparation, and various competitive examinations.

3. Can I enroll in more than one course?

Yes. Students can enroll in multiple courses simultaneously if they meet the eligibility requirements.

4. Are demo classes available before enrollment?

Yes. Selected courses provide free demo classes so students can experience our teaching methodology before enrolling.

5. How do I access my course after registration?

After successful registration and payment, log in to your account to access your course dashboard, study materials, lectures, and test series.

6. What payment methods are accepted?

We accept UPI, debit cards, credit cards, net banking, and other secure online payment methods.

7. Can I get a refund if I cancel my admission?

Refund eligibility depends on our Refund Policy. Please visit the Refund Policy page for complete details.

8. Are study materials included with the course?

Yes. Most courses include comprehensive study materials, practice sheets, assignments, and mock tests.

9. How do I contact Study Circle for support?

You can contact our support team through the Contact Us page, email, or phone during our working hours.

10. Will I receive updates about exams and classes?

Yes. Registered students receive important announcements, class schedules, and examination updates through their registered email and dashboard.
