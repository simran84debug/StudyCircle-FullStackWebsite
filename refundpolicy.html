Refund Policy

At Study Circle, we are committed to providing quality educational services.
Please read our refund policy carefully before enrolling in any course or test series.

Course Enrollment

1. Once a student has successfully enrolled in a course, the registration fee is generally non-refundable.
2. Refund requests for course enrollment may be considered under exceptional circumstances, such as medical emergencies or other unforeseen situations.
Such requests must be submitted in writing to our support team within 7 days of enrollment, along with valid documentation supporting the claim.
3. Refund requests made within 7 days of enrollment may be considered only if the student has not accessed a significant portion of the course materials.

Test Series

1. Test series purchases are non-refundable once the tests have been accessed or attempted.
2. If a student has not accessed any of the test series content, they may request a refund within 7 days of purchase, provided they have not attempted any tests.
3. If a technical issue prevents access to the purchased test series, our support team will resolve the issue or provide an appropriate alternative.

Duplicate Payments

If you are charged more than once for the same transaction, the extra amount will be refunded after verification.

Cancellation by Study Circle

In the rare event that a course is cancelled by Study Circle, enrolled students will receive a full refund or may choose to transfer their enrollment to another available course.

Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed within 7-10 working days. The refunded amount will be credited to the original payment method.

Contact Us

For any refund-related queries, please contact our support team through the More page.
