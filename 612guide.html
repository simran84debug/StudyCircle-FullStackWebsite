SMART STUDY TIPS FOR CLASS 6th TO 12th

Study smarter, not harder, by using active recall, spaced repetition, and the Pomodoro Technique. Test your memory instead of just rereading notes. Break learning into short sessions to stay focused and avoid burnout. Review older topics regularly so you do not forget them. Here is a simple way to boost your study habits:

1. Active Recall (Close the Book)

Do not just read your textbook over and over. It makes your brain lazy. Instead, read a section, close the book, and write down what you just learned on a blank sheet of paper. Imagine you are trying to explain the topic to a younger friend (this is called the Feynman Technique).

2. Spaced Repetition (Review Regularly)

Do not cram all your studying into one day. Your brain forgets things quickly if you do not review them. Instead, review your notes every few days. Use flashcards or apps like Anki to help you remember important facts and formulas.

3. The Pomodoro Technique

Studying for hours without a break makes your brain tired. Use a timer to break your work into short bursts. Step 1: Study for 25 minutes with no distractions (put your phone in another room). Step 2: Take a 5-minute break to stretch or drink water. Step 3: Repeat this four times, then take a longer 20-minute break.

4. Mind Maps

Draw pictures instead of just writing words. A mind map puts your main topic in the middle of the page. You draw branches to connect related ideas or facts. It gives your brain a visual picture that is easy to remember during a test.

5. Practice Old Exams

Reading is only half the battle. You need to practice solving questions. Find past papers or use your class question bank. Set a timer and pretend you are taking the real test. This helps you write faster and stops you from panicking on exam day.
